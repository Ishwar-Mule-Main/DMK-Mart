    42→## A.2 Project Background
    43→
    44→DMK Mart is a **trading and distribution firm** that **purchases finished products from manufacturers or distributors** and **sells them to B2B buyers and B2C walk-in customers** in bulk, packets, pieces, sets, or single units. The product range is focused on **plastic-manufactured goods** — chairs, buckets, kitchenware, household items, and industrial goods — currently 500+ SKUs and growing.
    45→
    46→The client currently operates with fragmented tools (spreadsheets, manual ledgers, paper orders) and requires a **single dedicated software platform** to manage:
    47→
    48→- All **orders** (purchase + sales)
    49→- All **finance** (double-entry bookkeeping, journals, ledgers, P&L, balance sheet)
    50→- All **inventory** (dual-stock: sellable vs. damaged/broken)
    51→- All **firm/company data** (multiple firms, each fully isolated)
    52→- **AI-assisted operations** (copilot, low-stock alerts, decision support)
    53→
    54→**Critical Architecture Constraint (NEW):** This software is for **a single owner only**. There is **NO multi-user system, NO roles, NO permissions matrix**. The **firm/company is the account**. The owner logs in once and switches between firms.

   165→## B.2 The Single-Owner Model (CRITICAL UPDATE)
   166→
   167→### B.2.1 Design Principle
   168→
   169→> **There is NO user management. There are NO roles. There are NO permissions. The FIRM is the account. One owner operates everything.**
   170→
   171→### B.2.2 Implications
   172→
   173→| Traditional ERP | DMK Mart ERP |
   174→|---|---|
   175→| Users, roles, RBAC matrix | **Removed** |
   176→| User login per employee | **Single owner session** |
   177→| Approval hierarchies | **Owner approves everything implicitly** |
   178→| Firm + User scoping | **Firm scoping only** |
   179→| `created_by` user tracking | **Replaced by firm context** |
   180→
   181→### B.2.3 Authentication Model
   182→
   183→- The owner has a **single credential set** (email + password) OR a session-based owner token.
   184→- After authentication, the owner sees a **Firm Switcher** in the header.
   185→- The active firm ID is stored in the session/global store and injected into every data query.
   186→- There is **no login screen per firm**; switching is instant.
   187→
   188→### B.2.4 Data Isolation Rule
   189→
   190→Every business entity is scoped by `firm_id` (company_id):
   191→
   192→```
   193→products, inventory, customers, vendors,
   194→purchase_orders, sales_orders, invoices, returns,
   195→journal_entries, ledger, chart_of_accounts, financial_year
   196→```
   197→→ All carry `firm_id`. The owner's active firm filters every query.

   342→## B.5 Inventory Management (Dual-Stock)
   343→
   344→### B.5.1 The Dual-Stock Model
   345→
   346→Every product tracks **two separate stock pools**:
   347→
   348→| Pool | Meaning | Can Be Sold? |
   349→|---|---|---|
   350→| **Sellable Stock** | Good-condition, approved stock available for sale | ✅ Yes |
   351→| **Damaged / Broken Stock** | Cracked, broken, transit-damaged, defective items in quarantine | ❌ No |
   352→
   353→**Hard Invariant:** Billing can only draw from **Sellable Stock**. The system must never allow selling from Damaged Stock.
   354→
   355→### B.5.2 Stock Movement Rules (Complete Matrix)
   356→
   357→| Event | Sellable Stock | Damaged Stock | Voucher |
   358→|---|---|---|---|
   359→| Purchase received (good qty) | ↑ increases | — | Purchase |
   360→| Purchase received (damaged qty) | — | ↑ increases | Purchase (quarantine) |
   361→| Purchase Return (damaged returned to vendor) | — | ↓ decreases | Debit Note |
   362→| Sales Order / Invoice | ↓ decreases | — | Sales + COGS |
   363→| Sales Return (customer returns broken items) | — | ↑ increases | Credit Note |
   364→| Internal damage (warehouse breakage) | ↓ decreases | ↑ increases | Stock Adjustment |
   365→| Write-off / disposal of damaged | — | ↓ decreases | Journal |
   366→
   367→### B.5.3 Low Stock Alert System
   368→
   369→- Every product has a **Low Stock Threshold**.
   370→- When sellable stock ≤ threshold, the system raises an alert.
   371→- Alerts appear as:
   372→  - **Dashboard notification/badge**
   373→  - **Popup / notification center alert**
   374→  - **AI copilot proactive alert**
   375→- Alerts show: product name, current stock, threshold, and suggested reorder quantity.
   376→- Optional one-click **"Create Purchase Order"** from the alert.
   377→
   378→### B.5.4 Stock Valuation
   379→
   380→- **Valuation method:** Weighted Average Cost (default).
   381→- Every stock movement is logged in an **Inventory Movement Ledger** (date, product, qty, direction, source document) for full auditability.

   385→## B.6 Purchase Workflow
   386→
   387→### B.6.1 Vendor Accounts (Manufacturer vs Distributor)
   388→
   389→When creating a **vendor account**, the owner selects the vendor type:
   390→
   391→| Vendor Type | Behavior |
   392→|---|---|
   393→| **Manufacturer** | Shows **only that manufacturer's own brand products** when selecting items for a PO. Brand-scoped selection. |
   394→| **Distributor** | Shows **all available products** (multi-brand), searchable by keyword. |
   395→
   396→**Vendor profile fields:** Vendor name, type (Manufacturer/Distributor), GSTIN, state code, address, phone, payment terms (e.g., Net 30), opening balance, outstanding payable.
   397→
   398→### B.6.2 Purchase Order (PO) Lifecycle
   399→
   400→```
   401→Create PO (select vendor, add products, qty, unit cost)
   402→   │
   403→   ▼
   404→PO Status: PENDING  ← can be edited or cancelled here
   405→   │
   406→   ▼
   407→Goods arrive at warehouse → Receive (GRN)
   408→   │
   409→   ├─ Enter received quantity
   410→   ├─ Split into: Accepted (good) vs Damaged
   411→   │
   412→   ▼
   413→PO Status: CONFIRMED / RECEIVED
   414→   │
   415→   ▼
   416→Auto-post to Accounting & Finance (with date):
   417→   • Inventory ↑ (accepted qty)
   418→   • Damaged stock ↑ (damaged qty)
   419→   • Vendor Payable ↑
   420→   • Purchase Journal + ITC (Input Tax Credit) recorded
   421→```
   422→
   423→**Key Rules:**
   424→- While **PENDING**, the PO can be **re-edited** (add/remove products, change qty) or **cancelled**.
   425→- Once **confirmed/received**, editing is locked; changes go through Purchase Return.
   426→- Every confirmation updates **inventory and finance with the transaction date**.
   427→
   428→### B.6.3 Purchase Return (Debit Note)
   429→
   430→When products are damaged or rejected:
   431→
   432→```
   433→Select PO / products → Create Purchase Return
   434→   │
   435→   ├─ Enter damaged quantity being returned
   436→   ├─ Reason (transit damage / defective / wrong item)
   437→   │
   438→   ▼
   439→Effects:
   440→   • Damaged Stock ↓ decreases
   441→   • Vendor Payable ↓ decreases
   442→   • Debit Note generated
   443→   • ITC reversed
   444→   • Journal posted (dated)
   445→```
   446→
   447→### B.6.4 Vendor Payments
   448→
   449→- Record payments to vendors (Bank/NEFT/UPI/Cheque/Cash).
   450→- Each payment: reduces Vendor Payable, posts Payment journal, updates vendor ledger running balance.
   451→- Supports **partial payments** and tracks outstanding.

   465→
   466→### B.7.2 B2C Counter Sales & Customer Directory
   467→
   468→A dedicated **B2C Walk-In Counter** handles fast over-the-counter sales.
   469→
   470→**B2C Customer Directory feature:**
   471→- The counter maintains a **directory of walk-in customers** (name + phone number).
   472→- When a customer comes to buy:
   473→  - Search by **name keyword** or **phone number**.
   474→  - If found → auto-fill their details.
   475→  - If not found → a **"Create New Customer" popup** opens; the owner enters name + phone and saves to the directory.
   476→- The B2C counter account shows a **separate Buyers List** section with all such customers.
   477→- Every B2C order updates the **finance and accounting** area (sales revenue, cash/UPI receipt).
   478→- **B2C customers have NO credit limit** (instant payment only).
   479→
   480→**B2C Checkout Flow:**
   481→```
   482→Add products → Select/add customer from directory → Choose payment
   483→   (Cash / UPI / Card)
   484→   → Confirm → Receipt printed → Sale posted to books
   485→```
   486→
   487→### B.7.3 Sales Order / Invoice Lifecycle
   488→
   489→```
   490→Create Sale (select customer, add products, qty, pricing tier)
   491→   │
   492→   ▼
   493→System checks:
   494→   • Sellable stock available?
   495→   • Credit limit OK? (for B2B credit sales only)
   496→   │
   497→   ▼
   498→GST calculated (CGST+SGST intra-state / IGST inter-state)
   499→   │
   500→   ▼
   501→Invoice generated (A4 statutory format, downloadable/printable)
   502→   │
   503→   ▼
   504→Auto-post to Accounting & Finance (dated):
   505→   • Sellable Stock ↓
   506→   • Receivable ↑ (credit) OR Cash/Bank ↑ (paid)
   507→   • Sales Revenue recorded
   508→   • Output GST liability recorded
   509→   • COGS posted
   510→```
   511→
   512→### B.7.4 Sales Return (Credit Note)
   513→
   514→When a customer returns products:
   515→
   516→```
   517→Select invoice / products → Create Sales Return
   518→   │
   519→   ├─ Enter returned quantity
   520→   ├─ Mark condition (broken/damaged)
   521→   │
   522→   ▼
   523→Effects:
   524→   • Returned qty → added to DAMAGED STOCK (not sellable)
   525→   • Customer Receivable ↓ OR refund
   526→   • Credit Note generated
   527→   • Output GST reversed
   528→   • Sales Returns contra-revenue posted
   529→```
   530→
   531→**Critical Rule:** Sales returns of broken/damaged items go to **Damaged Stock**, never back to Sellable Stock.

   535→## B.8 Pricing Engine
   536→
   537→### B.8.1 Five-Tier Pricing
   538→
   539→Every product has five price tiers (defined in B.4.1). The customer's assigned tier determines the base price used in B2B billing.
   540→
   541→### B.8.2 Quantity-Based Bulk / Packaging Pricing (Sales Only)
   542→
   543→This applies **only to the Sales / Customer / Buyers section**. Prices change automatically based on quantity and packaging format:
   544→
   545→**Packaging Format Discount Table:**
   546→
   547→| Packaging Format | Unit Multiplier | Discount |
   548→|---|---|---|
   549→| PIECE (single) | 1 unit | 0% |
   550→| PACKET | 5 units | 5% |
   551→| SET | 10 units | 8% |
   552→| BOX | 12 units | 12% |
   553→| CRATE | 24 units | 15% |
   554→| MASTER LOT | 50+ units | 20% |
   555→
   556→**Effective Price Formula:**
   557→```
   558→Effective Unit Price = Tier Price × (1 − Packaging Discount) × (1 − Manual Discount)
   559→Line Taxable Amount  = Round(Effective Unit Price × Quantity, 2)
   560→```
   561→
   562→**Example:**
   563→- Product base (Wholesale tier): ₹235.00
   564→- Customer orders 24 units (2 boxes) → BOX_12 format → 12% discount
   565→- Effective price = 235 × (1 − 0.12) = ₹206.80
   566→- Line amount = 206.80 × 24 = ₹4,963.20
   567→- Customer saves ₹676.80 by buying in bulk
   568→

   571→## B.9 Financial Accounting & Bookkeeping
   572→
   573→### B.9.1 The Double-Entry Foundation
   574→
   575→Every monetary event posts a **balanced journal entry** where:
   576→
   577→```
   578→Σ Debits = Σ Credits   (always, for every transaction)
   579→```
   580→
   581→This is the core invariant. The system never allows an unbalanced entry.
   582→
   583→### B.9.2 Chart of Accounts (Per Firm)
   584→
   585→Each firm has its own chart of accounts, grouped by class:
   586→
   587→| Class | Examples |
   588→|---|---|
   589→| **Assets** | Cash in Hand, Bank Accounts, Accounts Receivable (Sundry Debtors), Inventory Asset, GST Input Tax Credit (ITC) |
   590→| **Liabilities** | Accounts Payable (Sundry Creditors), GST Output Payable (CGST/SGST/IGST), TDS Payable |
   591→| **Equity** | Owner's Capital, Retained Earnings |
   592→| **Revenue** | Domestic Sales Revenue, Sales Returns (contra) |
   593→| **Expenses** | Cost of Goods Sold (COGS), Purchases, Freight, Operating Expenses |
   594→
   595→### B.9.3 Journal Voucher Types
   596→
   597→| Voucher | Trigger |
   598→|---|---|
   599→| **Sales** | Sales invoice posted |
   600→| **Purchase** | Purchase / GRN confirmed |
   601→| **Receipt** | Money received from customer |
   602→| **Payment** | Money paid to vendor |
   603→| **Journal** | Manual adjustments, opening balances |
   604→| **Contra** | Cash ↔ Bank transfers |
   605→| **Credit Note** | Sales return |
   606→| **Debit Note** | Purchase return |
   607→
   608→### B.9.4 Party Ledgers with Opening & Closing Balances
   609→
   610→Every **customer and vendor account** maintains:
   611→
   612→- **Opening Balance** (as of FY start)
   613→- **Running transaction history** (invoices, payments, notes)
   614→- **Closing Balance** (live, real-time)
   615→- **Balance type** — `Dr` (they owe us / receivable) or `Cr` (we owe them / payable)
   616→
   617→**This applies to ALL accounts** — every party shows opening and closing balances, updated by every order, return, and payment.
   618→
   619→### B.9.5 Financial Statements (Real-Time)
   620→
   621→| Statement | Description |
   622→|---|---|
   623→| **Trial Balance** | All account balances; Σ Debits must equal Σ Credits |
   624→| **Profit & Loss** | Revenue − COGS = Gross Profit; − Expenses = Net Profit |
   625→| **Balance Sheet** | Assets = Liabilities + Equity |
   626→| **Day Book** | Chronological list of all vouchers for a date |
   627→
   628→### B.9.6 Financial Year Management
   629→
   630→- Each firm operates on a financial year (e.g., 2025–26, April–March).
   631→- Year switching; opening balances carried forward.
   632→- Period closing to lock completed periods.
   633→
   634→### B.9.7 Accounts Receivable / Payable Aging
   635→
   636→- **AR Aging** — buckets 0–30, 31–60, 61–90, 90+ days for receivables.
   637→- **AP Aging** — same buckets for payables.
   638→- Supports credit control and cash-flow planning.

   642→## B.10 GST & Statutory Compliance
   643→
   644→### B.10.1 GST Calculation Logic
   645→
   646→**Place of Supply determines the tax split:**
   647→
   648→```
   649→If Seller State Code = Buyer State Code  →  INTRA-STATE
   650→    CGST = Taxable × (GST Rate / 2)
   651→    SGST = Taxable × (GST Rate / 2)
   652→    IGST = 0
   653→
   654→If Seller State Code ≠ Buyer State Code  →  INTER-STATE
   655→    IGST = Taxable × GST Rate
   656→    CGST = 0, SGST = 0
   657→```
   658→
   659→State codes are derived from the GSTIN prefix.
   660→
   661→### B.10.2 Invoice Requirements
   662→
   663→Every tax invoice includes:
   664→- Firm GSTIN, name, address
   665→- Customer GSTIN (for B2B), name, address
   666→- Invoice number (sequential, firm-specific) and date
   667→- HSN code per item
   668→- Taxable value, CGST/SGST/IGST breakdown
   669→- Total, round-off, and **Amount in Words** (Indian numbering: Lakhs/Crores)
   670→
   671→### B.10.3 Round-Off (Rule 545)
   672→
   673→Invoice totals are rounded to the nearest rupee; the difference is posted to a **Round-Off account**.
   674→
   675→### B.10.4 Input Tax Credit (ITC)
   676→
   677→- Purchases record ITC as an asset.
   678→- Purchase returns reverse ITC.
   679→- Support for GSTR-2B reconciliation (matching vendor filings).

  1127→## C.4 Business Logic Engines
  1128→
  1129→### C.4.1 GST Calculation Engine
  1130→
  1131→```typescript
  1132→function calculateGST(taxable: number, gstRate: number,
  1133→                      sellerState: string, buyerState: string) {
  1134→  if (sellerState === buyerState) {
  1135→    // Intra-state
  1136→    const cgst = round2(taxable * (gstRate / 200));
  1137→    const sgst = round2(taxable * (gstRate / 200));
  1138→    return { cgst, sgst, igst: 0 };
  1139→  } else {
  1140→    // Inter-state
  1141→    const igst = round2(taxable * (gstRate / 100));
  1142→    return { cgst: 0, sgst: 0, igst };
  1143→  }
  1144→}
  1145→```
  1146→
  1147→### C.4.2 Pricing Engine
  1148→
  1149→```typescript
  1150→function calculateBulkPricing(tierPrice: number, quantity: number,
  1151→                              manualDiscountPct: number = 0) {
  1152→  let format = 'PIECE', discountPct = 0;
  1153→  if (quantity >= 50)      { format = 'MASTER_LOT_50'; discountPct = 20; }
  1154→  else if (quantity >= 24) { format = 'CRATE_24';      discountPct = 15; }
  1155→  else if (quantity >= 12) { format = 'BOX_12';        discountPct = 12; }
  1156→  else if (quantity >= 10) { format = 'SET_10';        discountPct = 8; }
  1157→  else if (quantity >= 5)  { format = 'PACKET_5';      discountPct = 5; }
  1158→
  1159→  const unitPrice = round2(tierPrice * (1 - discountPct/100)
  1160→                                       * (1 - manualDiscountPct/100));
  1161→  const taxable   = round2(unitPrice * quantity);
  1162→  const savings   = round2((tierPrice * quantity) - taxable);
  1163→  return { format, unitPrice, discountPct, taxable, savings };
  1164→}
  1165→```

  1167→### C.4.3 Double-Entry Journal Posting
  1168→
  1169→Every transaction posts a balanced journal. Example — Sales Invoice:
  1170→
  1171→```
  1172→Dr. Customer A/R (or Cash/Bank)    = grand_total
  1173→Cr. Sales Revenue                  = taxable
  1174→Cr. CGST Output Payable            = cgst
  1175→Cr. SGST Output Payable            = sgst
  1176→Cr. IGST Output Payable            = igst
  1177→Dr/Cr. Round-Off                   = round_off delta
  1178→```
  1179→Plus COGS companion:
  1180→```
  1181→Dr. Cost of Goods Sold             = cost
  1182→Cr. Inventory Asset                = cost
  1183→```

  1565→## E.1 Firm Setup Workflow
  1566→
  1567→```
  1568→1. Owner logs in (single credential)
  1569→2. On first launch → "Create Your First Firm" wizard
  1570→3. Enter firm details (name, code, GSTIN, state, address)
  1571→4. Enter bank & contact
  1572→5. Select financial year + invoice prefix
  1573→6. Upload logo (optional)
  1574→7. Enter opening balances (cash, bank)
  1575→8. System seeds Chart of Accounts for the firm
  1576→9. Firm ready → Owner lands on Dashboard
  1577→10. To add another firm → Settings → Firms → [+ Create New Firm]
  1578→11. To switch → Header Firm Switcher → select firm
  1579→```
  1580→
  1581→**Outcome:** Each firm is a fully isolated workspace with its own products, parties, orders, and books.

  1623→

  1628→  → Select type: Manufacturer | Distributor
  1629→  → If Manufacturer → enter brand name (scopes product selection)
  1630→  → Enter name, GSTIN, state, phone, payment terms
  1631→  → Enter opening balance
  1632→  → Save
  1633→```
  1634→
  1635→### E.3.2 Create Purchase Order
  1636→
  1637→```
  1638→Purchase → Purchase Orders → [+ New PO]
  1639→  → Select vendor
  1640→    • If Manufacturer → product search shows only that brand
  1641→    • If Distributor → product search shows all products
  1642→  → Add products (typeahead search), qty, unit cost
  1643→  → System computes taxable + GST (by vendor state)
  1644→  → Save as PENDING
  1645→```
  1646→
  1647→### E.3.3 Edit / Cancel Pending PO
  1648→
  1649→```
  1650→PO Register → select PENDING PO
  1651→  → [Re-Edit] → add/remove products, change qty → Save
  1652→  → [Cancel] → enter reason → PO cancelled
  1653→```
  1654→
  1655→### E.3.4 Confirm Receipt (GRN)
  1656→
  1657→```
  1658→PO Register → select PENDING PO → [Confirm Inward]
  1659→  → Enter received quantity per line
  1660→  → Split: Accepted (good) vs Damaged
  1661→  → Confirm
  1662→  → System:
  1663→     • stock_quantity += accepted
  1664→     • damaged_stock  += damaged
  1665→     • vendor closing_balance += grand_total
  1666→     • vendor ledger row added (PURCHASE, Cr)
  1667→     • Purchase journal posted:
  1668→        Dr Inventory (subtotal)
  1669→        Dr ITC (tax)
  1670→        Cr Vendor Payable (grand total)
  1671→  → PO status → CONFIRMED
  1672→```
  1673→
  1674→### E.3.5 Purchase Return (Debit Note)
  1675→
  1676→```
  1677→Purchase → Purchase Returns → [+ New Return]
  1678→  → Select vendor + reference PO
  1679→  → Add damaged products + qty + reason
  1680→  → Confirm
  1681→  → System:
  1682→     • damaged_stock -= qty
  1683→     • vendor closing_balance -= return_total
  1684→     • vendor ledger row (DEBIT_NOTE, Dr)
  1685→     • Debit Note journal:
  1686→        Dr Vendor Payable
  1687→        Cr Purchases (taxable)
  1688→        Cr ITC reversal (tax)
  1689→```
  1690→
  1691→### E.3.6 Vendor Payment
  1692→
  1693→```
  1694→Purchase → Vendor Payments → [+ Record Payment]
  1695→  → Select vendor, amount, mode (NEFT/UPI/Cheque/Cash), UTR ref
  1696→  → Confirm
  1697→  → System:
  1698→     • vendor closing_balance -= amount
  1699→     • vendor ledger row (PAYMENT, Dr)
  1700→     • Payment journal:
  1701→        Dr Vendor Payable
  1702→        Cr Bank/Cash
  1703→```

  1707→## E.4 Sales Workflow (B2B)
  1708→
  1709→### E.4.1 Create Customer
  1710→
  1711→```
  1712→Sales → Customers → [+ Add Customer]
  1713→  → Enter city + firm name → system composes "City FirmName"
  1714→  → Enter GSTIN, state, phone
  1715→  → Assign pricing tier
  1716→  → Set credit limit + credit days
  1717→  → Enter opening balance
  1718→  → Save
  1719→```
  1720→
  1721→### E.4.2 Create Sales Order / Invoice
  1722→
  1723→```
  1724→Sales → Fast Billing → [+ New Sale]
  1725→  → Select B2B customer
  1726→  → Add products (typeahead), qty
  1727→    • System applies customer's tier price
  1728→    • Bulk/packaging discount auto-applied by qty
  1729→  → System checks:
  1730→     • Sellable stock sufficient?
  1731→     • Credit limit OK? (projected outstanding ≤ limit)
  1732→  → Compute GST (intra/inter-state by state codes)
  1733→  → Select payment mode (Credit / Cash / UPI / NEFT)
  1734→  → Confirm
  1735→  → System:
  1736→     • stock_quantity -= qty
  1737→     • customer closing_balance += grand_total (if credit)
  1738→     • customer ledger row (SALES, Dr)
  1739→     • Sales journal:
  1740→        Dr A/R or Cash/Bank (grand total)
  1741→        Cr Sales Revenue (taxable)
  1742→        Cr CGST/SGST or IGST (tax)
  1743→        Dr/Cr Round-Off
  1744→     • COGS journal:
  1745→        Dr COGS
  1746→        Cr Inventory Asset
  1747→  → Invoice generated → A4 print/PDF
  1748→```
  1749→
  1750→### E.4.3 Sales Return (Credit Note)
  1751→
  1752→```
  1753→Sales → Sales Returns → [+ New Return]
  1754→  → Select customer + reference invoice
  1755→  → Add returned products + qty + defect type
  1756→  → Confirm
  1757→  → System:
  1758→     • damaged_stock += qty  (NOT sellable)
  1759→     • customer closing_balance -= return_total
  1760→     • customer ledger row (CREDIT_NOTE, Cr)
  1761→     • Credit Note journal:
  1762→        Dr Sales Returns (taxable)
  1763→        Dr Output GST reversal (tax)
  1764→        Cr Customer A/R (grand total)
  1765→```
  1766→
  1767→### E.4.4 Customer Payment (Receipt)

  1772→  → Confirm
  1773→  → System:
  1774→     • customer closing_balance -= amount
  1775→     • customer ledger row (RECEIPT, Cr)
  1776→     • Receipt journal:
  1777→        Dr Bank/Cash

  1788→  → Select customer from directory:

  1800→        Cr CGST/SGST or IGST

  1802→  → Receipt printed
  1803→  → NO credit limit check (B2C is instant payment)
